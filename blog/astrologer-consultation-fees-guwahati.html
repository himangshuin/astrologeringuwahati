Astrologer Consultation Fees in Guwahati & Assam: What to Expect

By Himu · 5 min read

"How much does an astrologer charge?" is one of the most searched questions before booking a session — and one of the least transparently answered. Here's an honest breakdown of astrologer fees in Guwahati and Assam.

Why Astrology Fees Vary So Much

Fees depend on the type of session (tarot vs. full Vedic astrology vs. Vastu), the session length, and the astrologer's experience. A quick 3-card tarot spread naturally costs less than a 60-minute Kundli analysis with Dasha timing and written remedies.

Typical Session Types & What They Involve

  • Quick Tarot Reading — A focused 30-minute session on one specific question (love, career or a decision). See our current pricing for exact starting rates.
  • Full Vedic Birth Chart (Kundli) Reading — A detailed 60-minute session covering your full chart, Dasha timing and personalised remedies. This is the most popular option for career, marriage and major life questions.
  • Combined Session — Astrology + Numerology + Vastu together, typically for major decisions like marriage, a career shift or moving into a new home.

Red Flags to Watch For

Be cautious of anyone who won't quote a fee upfront, or who pressures you into expensive "special pujas" or rituals costing thousands of rupees after an initial low-cost or free reading. Ethical astrologers are transparent about pricing from the first message.
